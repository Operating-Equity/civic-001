Refund and Dispute Policy

Listing is free. A test that fails is never charged. A completed test is final, and faults are reviewed.

Effective date: [date]. This policy is part of the FactEngine Terms of Service.

Listing is free. Finding and listing the claims in a document never costs anything.

A test that fails is never charged. If a test does not complete, because of an error, a connection that was lost, an update to the Service while it ran, or because you stopped it, you are not charged for it, and it does not use up a free test.

A completed test is final. A completed test is a delivered service: the model has done the work and the result is yours. It is not refunded because you disagree with the verdict, because a source turned out to be wrong, or because you no longer need it. The price you see when you start a test is the price for that test.

Faults are reviewed. If you believe a completed test was charged in error, charged twice, or had a fault (for example, the result was empty or unreadable), write to support@operatingequity.ai within 14 days of the charge with the document, the claim and the date. We will review it within five business days and may, at our discretion, refund or credit the charge. A refund goes back to the payment method used.

Cancellation. There is no subscription to cancel. You choose each test, and you may stop using the Service at any time.

Chargebacks. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your card issuer; most problems are settled faster that way. A chargeback on a completed test may lead us to suspend access while it is resolved.

Contact: support@operatingequity.ai.
