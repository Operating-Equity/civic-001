Privacy Policy

What FactEngine collects when you use it, why, who sees it and what your choices are.

Effective date: [date]. This policy explains what Fact Engine LLC ("we", "us"), the operator of FactEngine at FactEngine.com, collects when you use the Service, why, who sees it and what your choices are. Questions and requests go to support@operatingequity.ai.

1. What we collect, and why

What When Why Kept
The email address you give at sign-in, and a one-way fingerprint of your access code (never the code itself) Each sign-in To know who ran a test, count runs against a code's allowance, and reach you about your use of the Service For as long as the Service keeps its records of use, then deleted
The documents, links, pasted text and claims you submit, and files you upload Each run To list the claims and test the ones you choose Held in memory only while the run is open; not stored afterwards

We collect nothing else. In particular, we do not collect your location, device identifiers or browsing history, and we do not place analytics or advertising trackers.

2. Cookies and browser storage

The Service sets one cookie, to keep you signed in, and stores one preference in your browser, your chosen language. Neither is used for tracking or advertising, and no third-party cookie is set by our pages. A video you test may play through the video platform's own embedded player, in its privacy-enhanced mode; the player may set its own cookies when you play the video, under that platform's privacy policy.

3. Who sees your information

We share information only with the categories of service provider below, each for its part and under its own terms. The providers may change; what each receives does not:

  • A model provider that lists the claims in what you submit: it processes the documents you submit and may search the web while doing so. It processes data in China, and its terms allow it to use a small part of the inputs it receives, de-identified, to improve its models unless we opt out.
  • A model provider that produces the determinations: it processes the claims you choose and the documents they come from, and performs web searches as part of a determination, under terms that do not permit your inputs to be used to train its models.
  • A search provider, which may receive the searches a model makes while listing claims, and the words of a link's own address and date when the page refuses our reader, to find a licensed copy elsewhere.
  • A transcript provider, which receives the address of a video when its transcript cannot be read directly.
  • The video platform whose embedded player shows a video you test.
  • A hosting provider, which runs the Service and its database in the United States.
  • A payment processor, which handles payment when payment is enabled; it holds your card details, which we never see.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

4. How we use it

To provide and operate the Service; to count use against a code's or an account's allowance; to measure what the Service costs and earns and to set its prices; to keep the Service secure and find faults; to answer your requests; to send you notices about the Service or your use of it; and to comply with the law. We do not use your documents or claims to train models, and we do not profile you for advertising.

5. Retention

Your documents, links and claims are not stored after the run ends. Records of sign-ins, runs and tests are kept for as long as we need them for accounting, the measurement of prices and the operation of the Service. Technical logs are kept for a limited time. When you ask us to delete your information, we delete the records linked to your email address except what we must keep for accounting or legal reasons.

6. Security

The Service is served over HTTPS; the sign-in cookie is signed and cannot be read by scripts; our servers hold the keys to the services we use and never send them to your browser; access to the operator's records is limited to the operator. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to support@operatingequity.ai from the email address you use with the Service. We will answer within 30 days. You may stop using the Service at any time.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland: we process your information to perform our contract with you (providing the Service), for our legitimate interests in operating, securing and pricing the Service, and to comply with the law; you also have the rights to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your supervisory authority. Our servers are in the United States, and the model provider that lists claims processes data in China; by using the Service you understand your information is transferred to those countries, and we rely on the standard contractual clauses or another lawful mechanism for those transfers where required.

If you are a California resident: you have the rights to know, delete and correct the personal information we hold, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the Service.

8. Children

The Service is for readers aged 13 and older. Readers aged 13 to 17 may use it with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has used the Service, write to us and we will delete the information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. We will post the new version with a new effective date, and give notice on the Service or by email for a material change.
